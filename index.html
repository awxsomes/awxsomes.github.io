What I'm doing at the moment

Three things take up most of my time this semester.

Sep 2026 — present

Backend work for the Agroecology Lab — App Dev Club @ UMD

I'm helping build an interactive map where the research team can draw and save land-parcel boundaries. The boundaries are stored as PostGIS polygons in a shared Postgres database on AWS RDS, and I'm writing FastAPI endpoints with teammates to connect the React frontend to that database. A lot of it is translating the team's ERD into a schema for parcels, goals, and practices that actually works.

Jun 2025 — present

Research at UMD — constrained models

I work on models that are forced to stay calibrated instead of just chasing accuracy. That includes a constrained neural network I built from scratch in NumPy/CuPy (with hand-written, CUDA-accelerated backprop) and a constrained random forest.
